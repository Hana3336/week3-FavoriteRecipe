Pancakes recipe

Ingredients

  • milk 250ml
  • eggs 2
  • a pinch of salt
  • sugar 3 tea spoons
  • wheat flour 200g
  • oil / butter

Instructions

  1. Pour the milk into a bowl, add the eggs, salt and sugar. Beat with a whisk until smooth.
  2. Gradually add the flour and continue beating until you have a smooth batter.
  3. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and brush it lightly with oil or butter
  4. Pour about a ladleful of batter into the hot pan and tilt the pan to spread it over the entire surface
  5. Fry until the top is completely dry and the thin edges curl up
  6. Flip the pancake over and fry it until golden on both sides
